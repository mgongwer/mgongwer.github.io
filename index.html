Background

About

I am an MD/PhD student at UCLA, where I completed my PhD in Neuroscience in May 2026 with Laura DeNardo. My research spans the cellular mechanisms of transcranial magnetic stimulation, microglial contributions to circuit development, and the brain-wide organization of pathways involved in threat avoidance.

I also developed DeepTraCE and DeepCOUNT, machine-learning pipelines for mapping axons and cell bodies in whole-brain light-sheet images.
